100% On-Device Document & Signature Processing

Signature Maker is an offline-first, local document signing and editing application. Your PDF documents, scanned camera pages, drawn signatures, stamps, dates, and text annotations are processed and stored exclusively on your device. We never upload, transmit, store, or view your documents or signatures on any external server.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Signature Maker ("we," "our," or "us"), published by CursorTek. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (Package ID: com.cursortek.pdf_signature_maker) handles information when you use our application on Android or iOS devices.

2. Information We Process

2.1. Documents, Signatures, and Annotations (Local Only)

When you import PDF files, capture pages with your camera, pick images from your gallery, or draw signatures:

  • Local Processing Only: All PDF rendering, text extraction/recognition, signature drawing, stamp placement, and PDF file generation execute directly on your device.
  • Local Storage: Saved signatures, downloaded custom fonts, and your saved PDF list are stored inside your device's private application storage using an on-device database (Hive) and local preferences (SharedPreferences).
  • Automatic Session Cache Cleanup: Temporary page images created while editing a document are stored in an isolated local cache directory (active_document_cache) and are automatically purged when you close the editor, save the PDF, or restart the app.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: We never transmit your PDF files, photos, or signatures to our servers or any third party.

2.2. Automatically Collected Technical Diagnostics

Our app uses Google Firebase Core (firebase_core) for foundational app initialization. Depending on your device settings, Google Play Services and Firebase may process basic technical device metadata (such as device model, OS version, or installation instance identifiers) strictly for service reliability and security.

3. Device Permissions We Request and Why

Signature Maker requests only the system permissions required to scan, import, edit, and export your PDF documents:

Permission Android Permission Name Purpose in the App
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Allows you to capture photos of physical paper documents inside the app to convert them into signable PDF pages. Camera images remain strictly on your device.
Storage / Photos & Files android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & System File/Photo Picker Allows you to import existing PDF files or gallery images into the editor, and to export or save signed PDF documents to your device storage.
Internet & Network State android.permission.INTERNET Used solely to download optional custom typography fonts (.ttf files from Google Fonts) when you select a new font in the Text Editor, and to check connectivity before downloading.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time via your device's Settings → Apps → Signature Maker → Permissions.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Signature Maker integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Core: Used for core application initialization. Read Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Fonts: When you choose to download an optional font style in the Text Editor, the app makes an HTTPS request to fetch the .ttf font file and caches it locally on your device for offline reuse.

5. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Control)

Because all of your documents and signatures are stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times:

  • Delete Saved PDFs: Open the Files tab inside the app to delete individual PDF documents or tap Delete All to permanently erase all saved PDFs.
  • Delete Saved Signatures: Open the Add Signature sheet in the editor to remove any saved signature.
  • Complete Removal: Uninstalling Signature Maker or clearing the app's storage in your device settings immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored PDFs, signatures, cached fonts, and preferences.

6. Data Security

All document rendering and PDF generation execute within your operating system's sandboxed application directory. Any external network requests (such as optional font downloads) are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
